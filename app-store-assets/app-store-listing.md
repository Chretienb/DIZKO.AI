## Description (4000 char max)
Dizko is where your music actually gets made — not just stored.

Upload a stem and Dizko auto-tags it with BPM and key, no manual naming ever. Drop it into the Studio timeline — a real multi-track arrangement view, built for mobile, so you can move clips, mute/solo tracks, and hear your mix update in real time.

Bring your whole crew. Invite collaborators to a project, see who's online, and everyone hears changes as they happen — no more "final_v3_ACTUALFINAL.wav" passed around over text.

Share your sound. Every artist gets a public profile — a showcase for your tracks, followers, and reposts — so people can discover your work without leaving the app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Auto-organized stems — BPM + key tagged on every upload
• A real timeline — multi-track arrangement, not just a file list
• Real-time collaboration — your crew hears changes as you make them
• Public artist profiles — showcase tracks, build a following
• Direct messages — line up your next collab without leaving Dizko
• Works alongside your DAW — export stems whenever you need them

Free to start. Upgrade for more storage, unlimited projects, and priority processing — manage your plan anytime at dizko.ai.

Questions or feedback? We're at team@dizko.ai.
